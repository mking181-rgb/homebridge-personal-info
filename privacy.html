Privacy information

This integration is used only by its owner. Google Nest device information, thermostat settings and sensor readings are accessed to display status and control the thermostat through Apple Home. Authorization credentials and bridge configuration are stored on the owner’s computer. Device updates are delivered through the owner’s Google Cloud project.

Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties. Google and Apple process data needed to provide their respective services.

The owner can revoke access in Google’s account or Nest connection settings and remove the bridge and its local data at any time. This integration has no public signup or external users.

These public information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process visitor connection information under its own privacy statement. No thermostat data or credentials are published here.
